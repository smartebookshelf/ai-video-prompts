## The 5-part video prompt formula

Most AI clips fail because the prompt is vague. Every prompt below has these 5 parts:

| Part | What to write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Subject** | Who or what is on screen | an elderly baker with flour on her hands |
| **2. Action** | What happens, one clear action | slowly pulls a golden loaf from a stone oven |
| **3. Camera** | Shot type and movement | close-up, slow dolly-in |
| **4. Light** | Time of day and light source | warm morning light through a window |
| **5. Style** | Look and format | cinematic, shallow depth of field, 9:16 |

**Tips that fix most bad clips**
- One action per clip. Two actions usually means a messy clip.
- Name the camera move. "Slow dolly-in" beats "cinematic".
- Set the format: **9:16** for Shorts, Reels and TikTok; **16:9** for YouTube.
- Keep clips short (5–8 seconds) and edit them together.

## Keep the same character in every clip

Write one **character line** and paste it into every prompt, word for word:

> A woman in her 30s with short curly black hair, round gold glasses, a mustard-yellow sweater and a small silver nose ring.

Then only change the **action, camera and setting**. Many tools also let you start from a reference image of the character, which keeps the face even more consistent.
